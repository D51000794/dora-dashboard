Caveats and how to read this

  • Release lead time isn't measured. Release runs are triggered manually with no commit metadata, so the API reports pipeline runtime instead. Only CI builds carry commit timestamps. Fix: pass the build's commit SHA and commit time into each release pipeline as variables.
  • MTTR is an approximation. It measures a failed run to the next successful run on the same pipeline, not incident detection to service restored. A failed run left unretried for days shows up as a long "recovery" and pulls the mean up.
  • Non-prod isn't production. DORA is defined against production. Non-prod releases and CI builds are shown as delivery health, not as DORA scores to benchmark. Only Banner has prod release pipelines in this data.
  • CI builds are not deployments. Their "deploys" are successful builds and their failure rate is build failures, so they are kept apart from the release groups.
  • Banner: prod releases through Harness began —, so quarter-wide prod rates understate the current pace. CU-Release-Pipeline's longest recovery, —, was an unretried failure, not an outage.
  • APG: failures concentrate in the Functions pipelines; APG-Functions-release-nprod has — failures never followed by a success. APG-Functions-build has no tier tag in Harness, and the prod build pipelines only ran in late August.
  • Financial Aid: activity mostly stops after August; the last successful release was —. finaid-functions-release-nprod fails most of its runs, with — failures never recovered.
  • Rating bands used. Deployment frequency per pipeline: Elite above 7 a week, High 1 a week or more, Medium monthly or more, Low below. Lead time: Elite under a day, High under a week, Medium under a month. Change failure rate: Elite 5% or less, High 10% or less, Medium 15% or less, Low above. MTTR: Elite under an hour, High under a day, Medium under a week, Low a week or more.
